## Notes
### Competitive Analysis
At *minimum*, we'll look at three competitors. For each, we want to measure:
- How many pages on the website?
- What kinds of pages do they have?
- How is their information organized? In other words, what's first, what's in the middle, what's at the bottom? (information hierarchy, basically)
- Measuring "work" (do keep note of any redirects or new tabs)
  - How much scrolling and many clicks to find the store location?
  - How much scrolling and many clicks to find prices?
  - How much scrolling and many clicks to make an appointment?
  - How much scrolling and many clicks to find contact information?
- To what extent does the website reflect the store's branding? Look at in person photos and the website's design and color scheme.
